### Added (M2, part 2: TRACK and tracking tools)
- `element_surrogate.track_adapter`: one TRACK field-map element (`sol3d`) run in an
  isolated directory; the installation, the staged field-file name and the constants that
  turn rigidity into beta*gamma are arguments; the aperture is given as a shape so TRACK's
  sign convention is written once; `read_dis.out` parsed in input order with its flags.
- Tools `fit_reference_orbit` and `check_tracking_convergence` (particles sampled inside the
  tool from half-ranges and a seed).
- `region.nominal_bend`: a nominal bend from tool arguments, used by regions and tools.

### Fixed (M2, part 2)
- A reference fit that cannot reach the exit says why the design particle stopped.
- Numbers written for TRACK are Python floats: a NumPy float's repr is not a number.

### Added (M2, part 1: tracking)
- `element_surrogate.lorentz`: Lorentz-force tracking through a static magnetic field,
  RK4 in path length on the unit direction with signed rigidity, entry and exit planes as
  path frames, steps shortened onto the exit plane and the last residual covered by one
  Euler step (position and direction), stop statuses (outside the field, outside a region,
  path limit, non-finite, wrong exit direction, step limit), direction-norm monitoring,
  `step_convergence`, and `UniformField` for analytic controls.
- `element_surrogate.reference`: reference orbits traced at a fixed field strength or
  fitted for a symmetric exit with every other coordinate's closure checked, frames along
  the orbit, and the orbit's departure from the nominal path.
- `FieldMap.contains` and `Region.contains` for points; `scipy` interpolation of orbits.

### Fixed (M2, part 1)

  the middle plane even where the band runs off the grid in x, and says which plane its

  with a band silently.

### Added (M1, part 4: completes M1)
- `element_surrogate.gradients` and the tool `analyze_generalized_gradients`: Venturini-Dragt
  generalized gradients from `B_rho` on a cylinder (linear or cubic sampling), exponential
  tails whose decay length is measured at each end, integrated multipoles, the change with
  the other sampling, and the map's interior against the field its surface implies; `scipy`
  is a dependency.
- `readiness.compare_exports` and the tool `compare_field_exports`: two exports of one solve
  compared at the first map's nodes, with the best scale and its sign, the difference, the
  trilinear reading's own error, each map's node floor and both maps' Maxwell residuals;
  opposite polarity is a finding, and so are a scale or a difference beyond a tolerance.
- `FieldMap.sample`: trilinear reading of a map at any points.
- `check_maxwell` (version 4) compares fourth- and second-order stencils in each shell.
- Corpus cases for two exports of one field: opposite polarity and another excitation.

### Fixed (M1, part 4)

### Added (M1, part 3: dipoles, CST exports and the corpus)

- `element_surrogate.corpus`: two clean synthetic magnets, ten planted defects and a
  runner; `synthetic.bend_sources`, `plant_gauss_as_tesla` and `plant_truncation`.

### Fixed (review of M0 and M1)
- Multipole fits are built in units of the fit radius: in metres the basis had a
  condition number of 3e17 and coefficients were silently wrong from order 8 up.

  real exports' node-level noise floor; clusters of bad nodes come worst first.

  (`load_field_file`); `read_track_field_table` is now `read_field_file`, and every
  field tool takes `format` and `slot`.
- `FieldMap` carries a `FieldSource`; `FieldFile` replaces `TrackTable`.
- Checks and fits take one shared `Region`; `harmonics` replaces `multipoles` and
  `profile`, fits once, and reports its sensitivity to the fit order.
- `isolated_zero_nodes` is now `zero_nodes_next_to_nonzero`.

### Added
- `readiness.storage_order` and the `check_storage_order` tool: the reader's storage
  assumption against every axis nesting, column assignment and sign, ranked by
  median vacuum-Maxwell residual.
- The harmonic-node check reports the map's noise floor, each node's ratio to it,
  and touching clusters of the top-ranked nodes.
- `synthetic.plant_storage_mismatch`.

### Added in M1, part 2
- `element_surrogate.readiness.harmonic_nodes`: each node's implied error (how far its
  value is from what its neighbours imply for a vacuum field, in tesla) and the
  order that separates a wrong value from truncation.
- `element_surrogate.analysis`: integrated multipoles fitted in a circle or a
  square, the main harmonic's profile with its integral, peak, centroid,
  map-profile length and edge fractions, and mirror parities of each component.
- MCP tools `check_harmonic_nodes`, `analyze_harmonics` and `check_mirror_symmetry`.

### Added in M1, part 1
- `element_surrogate.fields`: `FieldMap` (SI units) and a reader for TRACK Cartesian

  all-zero slots.
- `element_surrogate.readiness`: finite-volume divergence and curl on the map's own
  grid, shell reports about the z axis with a grid-coarsening comparison, and a

- `element_surrogate.synthetic`: exact vacuum fields of magnetic point sources and
