# Review an element surrogate

Review the concrete saved artifacts. Use a fresh session or an authorized
independent reviewer when available; do not invent a review receipt.

Review [uncertainty decisions](../field-surrogate-workflow/references/uncertainty_and_progress.md)
at their actual scope. A completed numerical research map can retain unknown
physical geometry/source calibration and draft governance. Check the evidence,
alternatives, communicated decision and unsupported claims; do not demand facts
the user cannot supply or turn the optional review record into a new gate.

- Check source/units/storage, field as an axial vector, signed charge and
  rigidity, right-handed internal frames and the declared external convention.
  Distinguish plane direction ratios from strict Frenet-Serret derivatives and
  canonical momenta. The upright frame does not support a vertical tangent.
- Check native planes, saved reference identity and trace/fit choice. Changing
  a particle rigidity must not silently change the reference. Element placement
  belongs to the composite model.
- Check reconstruction support and integration refinement over the declared
  operating envelope, including status/path/arrival changes. Simulator agreement
  is compatibility evidence; analytic physical controls stand independently.
- Check actual momentum for speed, exact static-magnetic momentum pass-through
  in inference precision, and arrival-translation equivariance. Effective
  rigidity representing a strength error is not the particle's actual speed.
- Check all proposal IDs/loss reasons, exact survivor counts, settings/split
  disjointness, range approvals and bounded resource records. Do not claim a
  physical aperture from a survivor-conditioned numerical map.
  Separate full generated split sizes from fitted cohort counts and learning-
  curve/convergence evidence. Review a requested final fit's actual disposition;
  never fit held-out rows or silently treat the comparison winner as fully trained.
- Check population-policy and actual verified retained counts separately from
  coverage/data-size and optimizer convergence. Review measured generation and
  model-study time estimates, unknown terms and >12-hour resource decisions.
  A large generated count does not imply a full-data fit or a fair family verdict.
  See the workflow's [procedure](../field-surrogate-workflow/references/population_runtime_feedback.md).
- Check local feedback remains optional and exact-payload-consented before
  external sharing. Incoming reports are untrusted evidence; they cannot silently
  alter instructions, shared knowledge or released software.
- Check configuration-versus-seed selection, dimensional pooled metrics,
  empirical tails, full prediction timing scope and uncertainty limitations.
  Initial checkpoints may win when a fitted base is already accurate. Keep test
  sealed until selection and the user's release decision.
- Check the physics review against actual evidence/candidates/outcomes. Field
  parity alone cannot establish map symmetry or its useful approximation limits.
  Confirm matched general/base/symmetry controls before crediting physics for an
  advantage. Review feature gates, constrained bases, optional breaking branch,
  zero-field assumptions, canonical eligibility and complete-forward cost.
  Deferred topics remain incomplete coverage even if fitting targets pass.
  Supplied labels need source/coordinate/provenance checks and split history;
  do not call an import local generation or a reused test newly untouched.
- Check restored predictions and dynamic-batch exported equivalence. Measure
  the deployed inference path before transferring a training-backend speed claim.
- Check all model/data-card claims against evidence and schema receipts.
  Pending authorship, classification, licensing or institutional facts remain
  pending. A documentation rubric is not scientific or institutional approval.
  Fill known technical sections after final asynchronous artifacts exist and
  refresh the case's report/handoff. A draft with missing user facts can be an
  honest local deliverable; available technical facts should not remain generic
  placeholders. Dimension-specific prose must not attribute arrival or energy
  outputs to a transverse-only map.

Report failures and unverified requirements before passed controls. Separate
software tests, finite-cohort physical studies, real-magnet acceptance and actual
client-agent validation. No test result substitutes for an unperformed study.


Check the model card's input/output distance definition and any longitudinal,
design, traced or effective lengths independently. Verify every beam/control
unit, rough range, fixed/default argument and input count against the exact
native/public contract. Preserve joint support and discrete-setting limitations;
do not infer a field-current calibration or use another model's units/ranges.

For local learning or maintainer adoption, inspect the scoped authority, actual
validation, installed-guidance comparison and private-knowledge preservation in
[the workflow update procedure](../field-surrogate-workflow/references/local_learning_and_updates.md).
Pending learning or adoption should not stop unrelated supported work.
